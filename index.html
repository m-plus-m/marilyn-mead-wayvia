The plan

Download as PDF
30
Days 1–30

Listen and align

Learn the business from customers, sellers and the team before changing anything.

  • Agree on the pipeline marketing owns with Sales and Finance: definition, monthly target and how it's measured.
  • One-on-ones with every team member to understand priorities, strengths and where they need clarity.
  • Listen to the market: Gong calls, 10+ customer conversations and win/loss reviews against key competitors.
  • Baseline the engine: funnel conversion, attribution and data flow across HubSpot, Salesforce, 6sense and Pendo.
  • Lock the Q1 event plan, starting with NRF in January: goals, owners, pre- and post-event follow-up.
By day 30Agreed pipeline target, marketing baseline and Q1 event plan.
60
Days 31–60

Build and pilot

Put one story and one scorecard in place, and start testing.

  • Every team member and agency partner owns a number: north-star goals translated into role-specific KPIs and agency scorecards.
  • One message architecture under Connect / Activate / Compete, with buyer-specific stories for brand marketers and ecommerce and channel leaders.
  • Enable sales on it, then check adoption in Gong.
  • ABM pilot with Sales on a short list of right-to-win accounts.
  • Nurture for qualified leads that don't convert yet, and marketing to every contact on open opportunities.
  • Customer advocacy pilot: customers as the heroes of their own success stories.
By day 60KPIs and scorecards in place, messaging live with Sales, ABM pilot running.
90
Days 61–90

Scale and prove

Grow from the customer base and set up 2027.

  • Customer lifecycle marketing with Customer Success, segmented by account tenure, size and role.
  • Customer advisory board launched with Product, feeding the roadmap and the community.
  • AI-native marketing: website built for shoppers and AI agents alike, plus AI workflows for content and lead routing.
  • 2027 plan and budget: in-house, agency, contractor and AI mix, backed into revenue targets with Finance.
  • Monthly marketing scorecard ready for leadership and the board.
By day 902027 plan and budget, lifecycle program live, board-ready reporting.
